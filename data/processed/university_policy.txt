# University Student Policies

- Policy scope: These policies apply to students enrolled in undergraduate courses.
- Policy status: This is fictional practice data for testing a retrieval system.
- Dataset version: 4.
- Attendance rule: Students should attend all scheduled lectures and laboratory sessions.
- Attendance exception: Students who miss a class should notify the instructor within 48 hours.
- Attendance responsibility: Students must obtain and review material missed during an absence.

- Late submission rule: An assignment submitted late loses 10 percent of its mark per calendar day.
- Late submission limit: Late work is accepted for a maximum of 3 calendar days.
- Late submission exception: An approved extension removes the late penalty.
- Extension request: Students should request an extension before the assignment deadline.

- Academic integrity rule: Students must submit their own work unless collaboration is explicitly allowed.
- Plagiarism rule: Copying words, code, data, or ideas without credit is prohibited.
- Cheating rule: Unauthorized help during an examination or assignment is prohibited.
- Academic integrity consequence: Suspected violations may be referred to the academic integrity committee.

- Artificial intelligence rule: AI tools may be used only when the instructor or assignment allows them.
- Artificial intelligence responsibility: Students are responsible for checking every AI-generated claim.
- Artificial intelligence disclosure: Students must acknowledge AI assistance when the assignment requires disclosure.
- Artificial intelligence privacy: Students must not upload confidential university or personal information to public AI tools.

- Grade review rule: A student may request a grade review within 7 calendar days of receiving the grade.
- Grade review requirement: The request must identify the rubric criterion and explain the reason for the review.
- Grade appeal rule: Formal appeals must follow the university appeal procedure after the grade review.

- Communication rule: Students must use their official university email for academic communication.
- Communication requirement: Messages should include the student name, course code, and a clear request.
- Response expectation: Staff normally respond during published working hours.

- Accessibility rule: Students who need accommodations should contact the university accessibility office.
- Accessibility privacy: Accommodation information must be shared only with staff who need it to support the student.

- Data protection rule: Student records must not be shared with unauthorized people.
- Data protection purpose: Personal information may be used only for legitimate academic or administrative purposes.

- Student conduct rule: Students must treat classmates, instructors, and staff respectfully.
- Student conduct prohibition: Harassment, threats, discrimination, and disruptive behavior are prohibited.
- Safety rule: Students must follow laboratory safety instructions and report hazards promptly.
