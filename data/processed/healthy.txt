HEALTHY HABITS AND SLEEP GUIDE

SECTION 1: SLEEP
Most adults need 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night. Consistent sleep and wake times help regulate the body's internal clock, known as the circadian rhythm. Avoid screens for at least 30 to 60 minutes before bed, because blue light can delay melatonin release. Keep the bedroom cool, dark, and quiet.

SECTION 2: HYDRATION
A common guideline is about 2 to 3 liters of water per day, depending on climate, body size, and activity level. In hot weather such as Jordan's summers, needs are higher. Signs of mild dehydration include dark urine, headaches, and fatigue.

SECTION 3: EXERCISE
Health organizations recommend at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week, such as brisk walking or cycling, plus muscle-strengthening exercises on two or more days. Even short 10-minute walks after meals improve blood sugar control.

SECTION 4: NUTRITION
A balanced plate includes half vegetables and fruit, a quarter lean protein (chicken, fish, legumes), and a quarter whole grains. Limit sugary drinks and heavily processed foods. Olive oil, nuts, and legumes are staples of the Mediterranean diet, which is linked to better heart health.

SECTION 5: STUDY AND FOCUS TIPS
Use the Pomodoro technique: 25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break. After four rounds, take a longer 15 to 30 minute break. Write down your top three tasks each morning and handle the hardest one first.

SECTION 6: STRESS MANAGEMENT
Deep breathing, short walks, and talking with friends reduce stress. Try box breathing: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Repeat for two minutes.
