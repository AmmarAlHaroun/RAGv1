RETRIEVAL METRICS

Recall & Precision

These metrics explain whether the retriever finds the right evidence and keeps irrelevant results out.

R

Recall

Of all the relevant chunks that exist, how many did we retrieve?

relevant chunks retrieved ÷ all relevant chunks

High recall: fewer important facts are missed.

P

Precision

Of the chunks we retrieved, how many are actually relevant?

relevant chunks retrieved ÷ chunks retrieved

High precision: less distracting evidence reaches the answer.

F

F1 score

A single balance between Recall and Precision.

2 × precision × recall ÷ (precision + recall)

Why: a high score means the retriever finds evidence without adding too much noise.

T

Test pass rate

The percentage of built-in questions whose expected evidence was found correctly.

passed test cases ÷ all test cases

Why: it gives an easy overall health signal for the current document set.

In practice: Recall helps prevent missing context; Precision helps keep answers focused; F1 shows their balance. The report also explains each test case so you can see why a percentage changed.
